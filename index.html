About The Event

A Platform for Ideas & Constructive Dialogue

NSU PODIUM 2026 is an official event organized by the North South University Debate Club (NSUDC).

Engaging Minds, Exchanging Perspectives

NSU PODIUM brings students together for discussion, debate, ideas, leadership and meaningful conversations.

Organized by NSUDC — North South University Debate Club, this platform encourages students to engage with diverse viewpoints, cultivate critical thinking, and build effective communication skills.

Through collaborative sessions, dialogue, and constructive exchange, participants explore contemporary topics while connecting with fellow students across the university community.
