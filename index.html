About NSU PODIUM

Organized by NSUDC — North South University Debate Club.

Leadership & Governance

Executive workshops on student club financial transparency, succession management, institutional advocacy, and sustainable event management.

National Club Showcase

Dedicated institutional pavilions to showcase your club's flagship projects, social impact campaigns, and technological innovations before national juries.

Inter-University Accord

Formal cross-campus bilateral networking sessions enabling clubs to sign collaborative MOUs, co-host nationwide events, and share knowledge resources.

Summit Itinerary (Day 1 & 2)

  1. 08:30 AM – 09:45 AM
    Institutional Delegation Accreditation
    Badge collection, delegation kits, and welcome tea at the Main Foyer.
  2. 10:00 AM – 11:30 AM
    Inaugural Plenary & Keynote Address
    Addresses by Vice Chancellors and guest industry keynote speakers.
  3. 12:00 PM – 01:30 PM
    Executive Roundtables & Breakouts
    Track sessions by club domain (Tech, Cultural, Debating, Leadership).
  4. 03:00 PM – 05:30 PM
    Club Innovation Expo & Pitch Sessions
    Presentations of top 20 collegiate community initiatives.
  5. 06:30 PM – 08:30 PM
    Delegates Networking Banquet
    Formal banquet dinner and inter-institutional recognition ceremony.

Registration & Delegation Guidelines

  • Institutional Affiliation: Each registering club must be officially recognized or affiliated with an accredited university or college.
  • Delegation Representative: The nominated representative serves as the primary liaison for summit accreditation, schedule updates, and logistics.
  • Slot Capacity: Clubs can request between 1 to 25 delegate passes. All delegate slots include full summit access, kits, and official certificates.
  • Identity Verification: Institutional Student/Faculty ID cards must be produced by all attendees during morning badge distribution.
  • Unique Reference Number: Save your assigned reference ID (`NCILS-2026-XXXXX`) to check confirmation status or modify delegation lists.
